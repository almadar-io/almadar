# Privacy & Cookies

_Last updated: 2026-06-02_

This notice explains how Almadar uses analytics and cookies across our sites
(almadar.io, orb.almadar.io, services.almadar.io, studio.almadar.io, and
masar.almadar.io) and the choices available to you.

## Analytics

We use **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** to understand how our sites and the Studio
app are used so we can improve them. When enabled, GA4 collects:

- pages/screens viewed and in-product actions (e.g. submitting a prompt,
  compiling, navigating);
- a pseudonymous client identifier and session information;
- technical data such as browser, device type, and approximate
  (coarse, IP-derived) location.

We do **not** use advertising features, and we do not sell your data. We send
GA4 only non-identifying metadata — for example the *length* of a prompt, not
its text — and never your prompt or message content.

## Cookies and similar storage

| Name | Purpose | Set when |
|---|---|---|
| `almadar_analytics_consent` | Remembers your analytics choice across our sites | Always (strictly necessary) |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics client/session identifiers | Only after you allow analytics |

The consent cookie is strictly necessary to honor your choice and is exempt
from consent. The Google Analytics cookies are set **only if you opt in**.

## Your choices (consent)

Analytics is **off by default**. The first time you visit, a banner lets you
**Allow** or **Decline**. Your choice is stored in a first-party cookie scoped
to `.almadar.io`, so it applies across all of our sites.

You can change your mind at any time by:

- **clearing the `almadar_analytics_consent` cookie** (and the `_ga` cookies) in
  your browser — you'll be asked again on your next visit; or
- installing Google's [opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout); or
- enabling "Do Not Track" / tracking protection in your browser.

For EEA/UK visitors, analytics runs under Google **Consent Mode**, defaulting to
denied until you opt in.
