CONCEPT: "Blueprint Decomposition - Isometric Stack"
Same decomposition idea but rendered as an isometric 3D stack. The finished app sits at the top as a flat screen, and below it the layers peel apart: Page layer, Trait layer, Entity layer. Each layer is a translucent isometric plane showing its contents. You're looking at the anatomy of an application from a 45-degree angle, like peeling apart a sandwich to see every ingredient.

LAYOUT:
- Full-width hero, centered composition
- Center: An isometric stack of 4 translucent layers, separated vertically with space between them:
  - TOP LAYER (highest): A finished app screen (dashboard UI, cards, sidebar) rendered as an isometric flat panel
  - LAYER 2: Page routes. Isometric plane with route cards ("/dashboard", "/settings", "/users") connected by navigation arrows
  - LAYER 3: Trait state machines. Isometric plane with 3-4 small state diagrams (circles + arrows), each labeled ("AuthFlow", "CartManager", "TaskLifecycle")
  - BOTTOM LAYER: Entity data shapes. Isometric plane with table-like blocks showing field names and types (User: name, email, role)
- Thin vertical dashed lines connecting elements across layers (e.g., a page connects down to the trait it renders, which connects down to the entity it manages)
- Title "Blueprints for your AI" positioned top-left or centered above the stack
- Subtitle below the stack

COLOR PALETTE:
- Background: Deep midnight blue (#0f172a)
- Layer planes: Semi-transparent dark slate (#1e293b at 80% opacity) with teal (#14b8a6) edge outlines
- Top app layer: Slightly brighter, more opaque, to show the finished product
- Vertical connection lines: Teal dashed, thin
- Entity fields: White monospace text
- State machine nodes: Teal circles with white labels
- Page route cards: Dark with teal left accent border
- Gold (#c9a227): Used for the "active" or "current state" indicator in one trait

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Title: 52px bold white
- Subtitle: 18px muted gray (#94a3b8)
- Layer labels (on the left side, labeling each plane): 14px uppercase, teal, monospace
- Content within layers: 10-11px monospace, white or teal

STYLE NOTES:
- The isometric angle should be consistent (true isometric: 30 degrees)
- Each layer should have a subtle shadow beneath it to emphasize the separation
- The transparency of lower layers gives a sense of depth without clutter
- The finished app on top should be the most visually rich and polished
- Subtle glow where vertical connection lines pass through each layer
- Light mode: White/light gray layers, dark text, same teal accents
