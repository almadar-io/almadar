CONCEPT: "Blueprint Decomposition - Construction Timeline"
The hero shows the application being built step by step, left to right, like a time-lapse of construction. On the far left: an empty canvas. Moving right: entities appear first (the foundation), then traits stack on top (the structure), then pages snap into place (the rooms), then UI patterns fill in (the furniture). On the far right: the complete, running application. Each phase is a snapshot, and you see the app gaining complexity and completeness as your eye moves across.

LAYOUT:
- Full-width hero
- A horizontal timeline strip across the center, showing 5 construction phases:
  1. EMPTY: A blank dark rectangle, labeled "Start". Just a cursor blinking.
  2. ENTITIES: The rectangle now has data tables/blocks appearing. Entity cards with field names. Foundation being laid. Label: "+Entities"
  3. TRAITS: State machine diagrams appear above the entities. Circles connected by arrows, showing behavior logic being added. Label: "+Traits"
  4. PAGES: Route cards snap into a navigation structure. A sidebar wireframe forms. The layout takes shape. Label: "+Pages"
  5. COMPLETE: The full, polished app. Colors fill in, real data appears, everything is alive. Label: "Verified. Shipped."
- Each phase is connected by a forward arrow (teal, with motion blur effect)
- Below the timeline: A progress bar showing completion (0% to 100%), with the "Verified" label at the end
- Title above the timeline
- Subtitle below

COLOR PALETTE:
- Background: Midnight blue (#0f172a)
- Timeline phases: Dark slate cards (#1e293b) with subtle teal border that gets brighter in each successive phase
- Phase 1 (empty): Nearly invisible, just a faint outline
- Phase 2 (entities): 25% opacity content, teal outlines
- Phase 3 (traits): 50% opacity, more detail visible
- Phase 4 (pages): 75% opacity, layout forming
- Phase 5 (complete): Full opacity, full color, polished
- Forward arrows: Teal gradient with motion blur
- Progress bar: Teal fill on dark track
- Gold (#c9a227): The final "Verified" checkmark at 100%

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Title: 52px bold white
- Subtitle: 18px muted gray
- Phase labels: 14px bold, teal, below each phase card
- "Verified. Shipped.": 16px bold, gold (#c9a227)
- Progress percentage: 12px monospace, teal

STYLE NOTES:
- The left-to-right progression should feel inevitable and satisfying, like watching something being built
- Each phase should be recognizably "more complete" than the previous one
- The opacity gradient across phases reinforces the construction metaphor
- The final phase should be noticeably more polished and colorful than the others
- Subtle animation suggestion: each phase could fade in sequentially on page load
- The progress bar anchors the bottom and adds a data-driven feel
- Light mode: White background, light gray phase cards gaining color/detail progressively
