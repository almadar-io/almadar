CONCEPT: "Decompose and Recompose"
A three-act story told left to right across the hero. Act 1 (left): A complete, polished application shown as a laptop/phone screen. Act 2 (center): The same app shatters apart into its orbital units, each floating independently as a self-contained card showing its own mini screen, state machine, and entity. Act 3 (right): The orbital units snap back together into a DIFFERENT application, showing that the same pieces can build something new. The message: applications are not monoliths, they're compositions of reusable orbital units.

LAYOUT:
- Full-width hero, three-act horizontal narrative
- ACT 1 - "Existing App" (left third):
  - A polished e-commerce app mockup (laptop screen). Sidebar with categories, product grid with cards showing images/prices/ratings, a cart icon with badge count in the top bar, search bar. Dark theme with teal accents. Clean, real-looking UI.
  - Small label below: "Your application"
  - Thin teal decomposition arrows radiating outward from the screen toward center

- ACT 2 - "Orbital Units" (center third):
  - 5-6 orbital unit cards floating in space, each one a self-contained piece:
    - PRODUCT UNIT: Mini card showing a tiny product list view at top, a small state machine below (states: browsing > viewing > filtering), entity fields at bottom (name, price, category, image)
    - CART UNIT: Mini cart view at top, state machine (empty > has-items > checkout), entity (cartItem: productId, quantity, price)
    - AUTH UNIT: Mini login screen at top, state machine (logged-out > authenticating > logged-in > error), entity (user: email, role, token)
    - SEARCH UNIT: Mini search bar + results at top, state machine (idle > searching > results > no-results), entity (query: term, filters, page)
    - ORDER UNIT: Mini order confirmation at top, state machine (pending > processing > shipped > delivered), entity (order: items, total, status, tracking)
  - Each unit card has three layers visible: a tiny app screen (top), a small state diagram (middle), entity fields (bottom)
  - Units float with slight rotation and depth, arranged in a loose cloud
  - Subtle teal glow connecting lines between units that share data (cart connects to product, order connects to cart)

- ACT 3 - "New App" (right third):
  - A DIFFERENT application assembled from the same units: an inventory management dashboard. Same building blocks (product, auth, search), different arrangement. Table view instead of grid, admin sidebar, stock levels instead of prices.
  - Small label below: "A different application"
  - Thin teal composition arrows flowing inward from the orbital units into this new screen

- FLOW INDICATORS:
  - Between Act 1 and Act 2: Curved arrows labeled "decompose" in teal
  - Between Act 2 and Act 3: Curved arrows labeled "recompose" in gold (#c9a227)

- Title "Blueprints for your AI" centered above, spanning all three acts
- Subtitle below: "Your product, defined and verified. Take back control."

COLOR PALETTE:
- Background: Deep midnight blue (#0f172a), darker at edges (#050810)
- Act 1 app screen: Dark app theme, teal accents, white text, product images as colored placeholder rectangles
- Orbital unit cards: Dark slate (#1e293b), teal left border, slight drop shadow. Each card approximately 200x280px equivalent.
- Mini screens within units: Darker background (#0a0f1a), simplified wireframe of the actual view
- State machine nodes: Teal (#14b8a6) filled circles (8px), white transition arrows, active state slightly larger
- Entity fields: White monospace text (9px), field types in muted gray
- Act 3 app screen: Same dark theme but different layout/arrangement, showing reuse
- Decompose arrows: Teal (#14b8a6), dashed, with slight spread effect
- Recompose arrows: Gold (#c9a227), solid, with convergence effect
- Connection lines between units: Very faint teal at 15%, dashed

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Title: 52px bold white, centered
- Subtitle: 18px muted gray (#94a3b8)
- "decompose" / "recompose" labels: 14px italic, teal and gold respectively
- "Your application" / "A different application": 13px, muted gray, below each app screen
- Orbital unit titles: 11px bold, teal, at top of each unit card (e.g., "ProductUnit", "CartUnit")
- State names in diagrams: 8px monospace, white
- Entity fields: 9px monospace, white for name, gray for type
- Mini screen wireframe labels: 8px, white at 60% opacity

STYLE NOTES:
- The three-act structure reads like a comic strip or storyboard: beginning, transformation, result
- Act 2 (center) should feel like an explosion in slow motion, pieces frozen mid-flight
- The orbital units should each feel complete and self-contained, like lego bricks you could pick up
- The fact that Act 3 is a DIFFERENT app from Act 1 is the key insight: same pieces, different product
- Each unit's mini screen should be recognizable as a simplified version of its corresponding part in Act 1
- The state machines should be simple (3-5 states) and readable at a glance
- Slight perspective on the floating units (some closer/larger, some further/smaller) gives depth
- The decompose arrows feel like things flying apart; the recompose arrows feel like things snapping together
- Light mode variant: White background, light gray unit cards, dark text, same teal/gold accents
