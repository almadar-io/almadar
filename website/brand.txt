Modern Heritage: A Global Tech Brand Strategy
The Almadar Brand Guidelines explicitly shifted away from "Islamic/Sci-Fi ornamentation" to a "Minimalist Document System" to ensure the brand looks like a global technology platform rather than a regional agency.
**However, we can honor the heritage subtly.**We can achieve a "Saudi-inspired" feel using abstract geometry (referencing modern Riyadh architecture or Sadu weaving patterns) while keeping the strict minimalist tech aesthetic. This aligns with the "Clean Arch" concept defined in your guidelines.
Here is a revised prompt that blends Saudi Heritage geometry with your Global Tech identity:
Revised Design Specification: "Modern Heritage"
• Concept: "Digital Mashrabiya." Instead of plain rectangles, we use geometric grids that echo modern Saudi architecture (like the KAFD district), but rendered in flat, clean tech lines.
• Palette:
    ◦ Background: White/Parchment (#f8fafc).
    ◦ Lines: Teal (#14b8a6).
    ◦ Solids: Midnight Blue (#0f172a).
    ◦ Accent: A single thin line of Sand Gold (#c9a227) to hint at the desert landscape without dominating.
