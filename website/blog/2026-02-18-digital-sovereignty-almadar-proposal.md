Digital sovereignty has become a strategic priority across the Arab world and beyond. Nations are investing billions in AI, cloud infrastructure, and homegrown technology platforms — recognizing that dependence on foreign tech ecosystems is a vulnerability, not just an inconvenience.

This post presents Almadar's proposal: a practical framework for communities, institutions, and nations to own their digital infrastructure — from AI agents to full-stack applications — without starting from zero.
